THE CLINICAL PROBLEM

SLIDE 02 / 11

The Reactive Trap in Diabetes Care

Retrospective Care Trap

  • 422M+ patients worldwide suffer from diabetes (over 90% T2D).
  • Traditional CGMs are reactive: alarms ring only after acute hyperglycemia (>180 mg/dL) is already underway.

The Black-Box & Counterfactual Void

  • Existing AI models act as black boxes with no physiological transparency.
  • Clinicians cannot test: "What happens if the patient eats 45g carbs now vs walks for 30 minutes?"

THE GLUCOTWIN-X SOLUTION

SLIDE 03 / 11

A Multimodal, Continuously Updating Cyber-Physical Twin

1. Multimodal Ingestion

Fuses static EHR demographics (Synthea) with 1-min wearable telemetry (CGMacros CGM, meals, HR, steps) across 14 T2D cohorts.

2. Hybrid AI Architecture

Combines machine learning (+120m XGBoost forecast) with biophysical differential equations (Bergman Minimal ODE What-If simulator).

3. Live 3D Visualizer

Doctor Cockpit (React 19) + in-browser 3D WebGL Hologram + Unity 3D engine with photorealistic organs and live particle streams.

EXPLAINABLE AI (TreeSHAP)

SLIDE 06 / 11

Unmasking the "Why" Behind Risk Transitions

Live Feature Attribution

  • Calculates exact Shapley additive values for every 5-minute prediction.
  • Eliminates black-box distrust by showing clinicians the primary drivers of impending spikes.

Primary Discovered Drivers

  • cgm_rolling_mean_30: Recent metabolic momentum.
  • meal_carbs_window_60: Postprandial carb surge.
  • steps_sum_60 / hr_mean_60: Protective physical activity buffer.

WHAT-IF SIMULATION LAB

SLIDE 07 / 11

Mechanistic Glucose-Insulin Surrogate (Bergman Minimal ODE)

dG/dt = -(p1 + X)·G + p1·Gb + D(t) - E(t)   |   dX/dt = -p2·X + p3·(I - Ib)

RK45 Numerical Integrator

Executed dynamically via SciPy `solve_ivp` to simulate physiological trajectories within milliseconds.

Clinical Intervention Testing

Allows doctors to drag sliders (e.g. +40g meal vs 30 min exercise) and immediately compare alternative metabolic futures against baseline.
