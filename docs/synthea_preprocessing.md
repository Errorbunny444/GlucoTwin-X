# Synthea EHR Preprocessing & Static Digital Patient Profiles

## Overview
This document details the preprocessing pipeline that transforms raw synthetic longitudinal Electronic Health Records (EHR) generated by **Synthea** into clean, compact, and traceable **static digital patient profiles** for the **GlucoTwin-X** platform.

---

## 1. Input Datasets
The pipeline ingests raw synthetic records from the verified Synthea dataset located at `data/raw/synthea/`:
- `patients.csv`: Core patient demographics, birthdate, death date, race, ethnicity, and gender.
- `encounters.csv`: Longitudinal clinical visits, outpatient checks, and inpatient admissions.
- `conditions.csv`: Historical and active diagnoses coded in SNOMED-CT.
- `observations.csv`: Physiological vitals, clinical laboratory panels, and biomarkers coded in LOINC.
- `medications.csv`: Outpatient drug prescriptions and dispensations coded in RxNorm.

> **Integrity Rule**: Raw data files are strictly treated as read-only and are never modified or altered in place.

---

## 2. Processing Pipeline

The pipeline follows a modular architecture implemented in [`src/glucotwin/preprocessing/synthea.py`](file:///c:/Users/acer/Downloads/Healthcare%20Digital%20Twin/GlucoTwin-X/src/glucotwin/preprocessing/synthea.py) and executed via [`scripts/preprocess_synthea.py`](file:///c:/Users/acer/Downloads/Healthcare%20Digital%20Twin/GlucoTwin-X/scripts/preprocess_synthea.py):

### 2.1 Identity and PII Handling
- Preserves the synthetic identifier as `source_patient_id` for full end-to-end traceability.
- Completely drops non-clinical direct identifiers (e.g., `FIRST`, `LAST`, `SSN`, `DRIVERS`, `PASSPORT`, `ADDRESS`, `PHONE`) to maintain a clean, compact schema.

### 2.2 Demographics & Age Determination
- Standardizes demographic columns: `gender`, `race`, `ethnicity`.
- **Reproducible Age Calculation**:
  - Rather than using the real-world execution date (which would artificially age historical patient snapshots), age is calculated relative to a **clinical reference date**:
    - **Deceased patients**: Reference date is `DEATHDATE` (capturing exact age at death).
    - **Living patients**: Reference date is their latest clinical encounter (`START` timestamp from `encounters.csv`).
    - **Fallback**: Latest clinical observation timestamp or global dataset snapshot date (`2020-04-01`).
  - Age formula: $\text{age} = \frac{\text{reference\_date} - \text{birthdate}}{365.25}$, rounded to 1 decimal place.

### 2.3 Disease Flags
Standardized boolean condition flags:
- `has_t2d`: True **only** for explicit Type-2 Diabetes Mellitus.
- `has_diabetes_related_condition`: True for any diabetes, prediabetes, or hyperglycemia.
- `has_hypertension`: True for hypertension.
- `has_obesity`: True for BMI 30+ obesity or severe obesity.
- `has_dyslipidemia`: True for hyperlipidemia/dyslipidemia.

### 2.4 Core Metabolic Observations & Robust LOINC Identification

### 2.5 Medication Representation
- `has_diabetes_medication`: Boolean indicating whether any diabetes prescription exists.
- `has_metformin`: Boolean for metformin prescriptions (`RxNorm 860975`).
- `has_insulin`: Boolean for insulin prescriptions (`RxNorm 106892`, `865098`).
- `diabetes_medication_count`: Distinct count of active diabetes drug classes (0 = none, 1 = monotherapy, 2 = dual therapy).
- `total_diabetes_prescriptions`: Cumulative lifetime prescription fill events.

## 3. Type-2 Diabetes (T2D) Cohort Definition

Explicit Type-2 Diabetes (`has_t2d`) is strictly defined and matched via:
1. **Specific SNOMED-CT Diagnosis Codes**:
   - `368581000119106`: Neuropathy due to type 2 diabetes mellitus (disorder)
   - `1551000119108`: Nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy due to type 2 diabetes mellitus (disorder)
   - `90781000119102`: Microalbuminuria due to type 2 diabetes mellitus (disorder)
   - `97331000119101`: Macular edema and retinopathy due to type 2 diabetes mellitus (disorder)
2. **Explicit Text Pattern Matching**:
   - Regular expression matching `type 2 diabetes` or `diabetes mellitus type 2` (case-insensitive).

> **Exclusion Criteria**: Patients exhibiting only generic hyperglycemia, gestational diabetes, prediabetes (`15777000`), or unspecified diabetes (`44054006`) without explicit Type-2 criteria are **not** flagged as `has_t2d`.
>
> **Target Match**: Yields **exactly 33 patients**, matching the verified baseline.

---

## 4. Observation Selection Rule

For each metabolic variable, repeated measurements over time are reduced to a single value by selecting the **latest valid observation** (chronologically sorted by timestamp).
- Both the measurement value (e.g., `hba1c`) and the ISO date of measurement (e.g., `hba1c_date`) are stored in the profile.
- Measurements from distinct clinical encounters are not averaged together, preserving point-in-time clinical accuracy.

---

## 5. Zero-Imputation Policy

Missing observations (e.g., patients without an HbA1c test) are **intentionally preserved as `NaN`/null**.
- **No mean imputation**
- **No median imputation**
- **No forward filling across population**
- **No fabricated values**

All imputation and feature engineering strategies are deferred to the downstream ML modeling phase.

---

## 6. Profile Completeness Metric

Profile quality is quantified by a simple, transparent metric:
$$\text{profile\_completeness} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{9} \mathbb{I}(\text{feature}_i \text{ is not null})}{9}$$
Evaluated across: `bmi`, `hba1c`, `glucose`, `systolic_bp`, `diastolic_bp`, `total_cholesterol`, `hdl`, `ldl`, and `triglycerides`.

- **Full Population Mean**: $0.6778$ (Median: $0.7778$)
- **Explicit T2D Cohort Mean**: **$1.0000$ (100% complete)**
